A Message from Lukas

After this page you can pull the four key facts (Who? What? When? Where?) out of short German text messages and invitations. After school Peter receives this message from Lukas.

Message on Peter's phone

'Hi Peter! Wir gehen heute Nachmittag um 16:00 Uhr in den Stadtpark zum Fußballspielen. Nadia kommt auch mit! Bring bitte Wasser mit. Treffpunkt ist am Eingang Süd. Bis später, Lukas'

Exercise

Where are Peter, Lukas, and Nadia meeting this afternoon?

  • At the south entrance of the town park (Eingang Süd) right answer

    'Treffpunkt ist am Eingang Süd' names the exact meeting point.

  • At Lindenberg station

    The station is not mentioned in Lukas's message.

  • At school

    Lukas writes about the town park, not school.

The signal word 'Treffpunkt' (meeting point) points straight to the location in invitations.
