Pass On Only the Key Facts

Peter's cousin Alex (12) from England is visiting with his parents for the weekend! On the school notice board hangs this poster. You do not need to translate every word — just pass on the key facts Alex wants to know.

Exercise

Alex (12) asks: 'How much is a ticket for kids our age?' What should Peter tell him?

  • 3 euros for kids up to 14 (5 euros for adults). right answer

    'Kinder (bis 14): 3,00 €' means 3 euros for kids up to 14 and 5 euros for adults.

  • It is free for kids.

    The poster lists 3,00 €, not free admission.

  • It costs 18 euros.

    18 is the date (18 October), not the ticket price.

On German posters, separate the date (18. Okt.), the time (17:00 Uhr), and the price (3,00 € / 5,00 €).

Relay Every Fact Clearly

Exercise

A German school-concert poster reads: 'Einlass: 16:30 Uhr · Beginn: 17:00 Uhr'. Which two time facts should Peter relay to Alex?

  • Doors open at 4:30 p.m. right answer

    Einlass (16:30) means when the doors open.

  • The concert starts at 5:00 p.m. right answer

    Beginn (17:00) gives the start time of the concert.

  • The concert ends at 5:00 p.m.

    17:00 is the start time, not the end.

German event posters almost always list Einlass (doors open) and Beginn (start) side by side.
