Dinner at the Webers'

At dinner with the Webers, Mina (9) suddenly sneezes — and Lukas calls out a German word right away!

Exercise

Mina sneezes at the table. What do people say in Germany?

  • Gesundheit! right answer

    Gesundheit! (literally 'health!') is the standard response to a sneeze.

  • Guten Appetit!

    Guten Appetit! is said before a meal, not after a sneeze.

  • Entschuldigung!

    Entschuldigung! is used when excusing yourself or apologizing.

Saying Gesundheit! after a sneeze and Guten Appetit! before eating is part of everyday life in Germany.

Respond Politely

Exercise

Match each situation to the right German phrase.

Choose the match for each entry.

Right answer: You thank someone for a gift. – Vielen Dank!; You arrive five minutes late. – Es tut mir leid.; You start eating dinner together. – Guten Appetit!; Someone says 'Danke!' to you. – Bitte schön!

Use 'Es tut mir leid' for a genuine apology and 'Entschuldigung' to get attention or pass by.

Exercise

Choose the right German phrase for each scene in Lindenberg.

  1. You want to get past someone on the bus. → …, darf ich vorbei?
  2. Frau Weber hands you a glass of water and you reply. → …, Frau Weber!
  3. Lukas thanks you for helping with his bike. → …, gern geschehen!
Solution
  1. Entschuldigung, darf ich vorbei?
  2. Vielen Dank, Frau Weber!
  3. Bitte schön, gern geschehen!

Bitte schön replies to Danke, and Entschuldigung politely gets someone's attention.
