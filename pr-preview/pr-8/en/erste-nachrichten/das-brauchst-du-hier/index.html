Before you write your own messages, one quick rule — Verb-second word order: the verb loves spot #2! I can place the conjugated verb in second position in German statements, including when time or place comes first.

Quick mental check

Compare both sentences: Ich gehe um 15 Uhr ins Eiscafé. → Um 15 Uhr gehe ich ins Eiscafé. The verb (gehe) always stays in spot #2!
