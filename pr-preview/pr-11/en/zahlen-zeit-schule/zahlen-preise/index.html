Bread Rolls and Bus Tickets in Lindenberg

After this page you can understand German numbers 0–100, prices in euros and cents, and phone numbers. In the morning Peter buys two Brötchen (bread rolls) at the bakery and then gets a ticket on the school bus. From 21 upward, German speaks ones first, then tens.

Exercise

A bus ticket costs 2,45 € (two euros forty-five). How do you say 45 in German?

  • fünfundvierzig right answer

    Ones first (fünf), then 'und', then tens (vierzig).

  • vierundfünfzig

    vierundfünfzig is 54 — a classic digit swap!

  • vierzigfünf

    German puts ones before tens linked by 'und'.

From 21 up, German always names the ones digit before the tens digit.

Peter buys a ticket for 'drei Euro zwanzig' and a roll for 'achtzig Cent'. How many euros does Peter pay in total?

Right answer: 4 €
