Four Signal Words at the Station

Weekend zoo trip with the Webers! Station loudspeakers move fast — focus on four signal words: Gleis (platform), Abfahrt (departure), time, and Verspätung (delay).

Loudspeaker announcement at Lindenberg station

'Achtung an Gleis 4: Der Regionalexpress nach Frankfurt, planmäßige Abfahrt 14:20 Uhr, hat heute leider 10 Minuten Verspätung und fährt heute von Gleis 6 ab. Ich wiederhole: Gleis 6!'

Exercise

Which platform does the train to Frankfurt actually leave from today?

  • Platform 6 (Gleis 6) right answer

    The train was moved from platform 4 to platform 6.

  • Platform 4 (Gleis 4)

    Platform 4 was the original platform — the announcement changes it to 6.

  • Platform 10 (Gleis 10)

    10 is the minutes of delay, not the platform number.

In German station announcements, always check the final platform number after 'fährt heute von … ab'.
