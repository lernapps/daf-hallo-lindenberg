### Four Signal Words at the Station

Weekend zoo trip with the Webers! Station loudspeakers move fast — focus on **four signal words**: **Gleis** (platform), **Abfahrt** (departure), **time**, and **Verspätung** (delay).

Picture: Departure board at Lindenberg station showing example train RE 30 to Kassel Hbf, departure 13:15, +5 min delay, platform 2, and the four signal words. Caption: Peter's Notebook: the four signal words on a German departure board.

'Achtung an Gleis 4: Der Regionalexpress nach Frankfurt, planmäßige Abfahrt 14:20 Uhr, hat heute leider 10 Minuten Verspätung und fährt heute von Gleis 6 ab. Ich wiederhole: Gleis 6!'

### Exercise durchsage-gleis

- Task: Which platform does the train to Frankfurt actually leave from today?
- Answer format: choose one answer
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ Platform 6 (Gleis 6) – The train was moved from platform 4 to platform 6.
  - ✗ Platform 4 (Gleis 4) – Platform 4 was the original platform — the announcement changes it to 6.
  - ✗ Platform 10 (Gleis 10) – 10 is the minutes of delay, not the platform number.
- Solution: Platform 6 (Gleis 6)
- Explanation: In German station announcements, always check the final platform number after 'fährt heute von … ab'.

### Exercise durchsage-zeit

- Task: With the delay, the train to Frankfurt departs at 14:30 (2:30 p.m.) today.
- Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
- Solution: The statement is true.
- Explanation: Scheduled departure at 14:20 plus a 10-minute delay equals 14:30.

### Exercise durchsage-verspaetung

- Task: A station announcement says: 'Der Zug nach Marburg hat fünfzehn Minuten Verspätung.' How many minutes late is the train?
- Answer format: type a number (minutes)
- Solution: 15 minutes
- Explanation: fünfzehn = 15 minutes of delay.
