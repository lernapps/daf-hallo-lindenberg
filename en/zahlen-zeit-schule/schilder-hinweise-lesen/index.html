After this page you can read German opening hours and everyday signs at a glance. You do not need every word — scan first for day abbreviations (Mo–Fr, Sa, So), 24-hour times, and geöffnet / geschlossen.

Exercise

Peter wants to borrow a book on Saturday at 2:00 p.m. (14:00). Can Peter go inside?

  • No, on Saturdays the library closes at 1:00 p.m. (13:00). right answer

    The sign says Sa 09:00–13:00, so at 14:00 it is already closed.

  • Yes, it is open until 6:00 p.m. every day.

    Until 18:00 applies only Monday to Friday (Mo–Fr).

  • No, it is closed all day on Saturdays.

    It is only closed all day on Sundays and public holidays.

In Germany, many shops and libraries have shorter Saturday hours and close on Sundays.

Recognize Everyday German Signs

Common signs in German towns
GermanEnglish
der Eingang / der Ausgangthe entrance / the exit
Eintritt freifree admission
Kein ZutrittNo entry
der Feiertag / die Feiertagethe public holiday / public holidays

Exercise

Which three German signs show a way in/out or free access (not a prohibition)?

  • Eingang right answer

    Eingang marks the entrance.

  • Ausgang right answer

    Ausgang marks the exit.

  • Kein Zutritt

    'Kein Zutritt' means No entry — you are not allowed in.

  • Eintritt frei right answer

    Eintritt frei means admission is free.

Eingang, Ausgang, and Eintritt frei appear everywhere across German towns.

Solution

    Eingang marks the entrance.

  • Ausgang right answer

    Ausgang marks the exit.
