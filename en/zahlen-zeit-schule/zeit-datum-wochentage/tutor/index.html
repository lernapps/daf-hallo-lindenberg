Context: the page “Time, Days, Months and Dates”

Tell formal and informal time in Germany and name days of the week, months, and dates.

Watch Out for 'halb drei'!

After this page you can tell formal and informal time and use days, months, and dates in German. Lukas tells Peter: 'Wir treffen uns um halb drei im Stadtpark.' In German, halb always looks forward to the upcoming hour!

Clock Times, Weekdays and Months

Picture: Analog clock showing halb looking forward to the next hour, plus memory box: um + clock time, am + day/date, im + month. Caption: Peter's Notebook: halb looks forward, and um/am/im structure schedules.

Official 24-hour time vs. everyday time:

  • Clock | Official (24h) | Everyday
  • 08:15 | acht Uhr fünfzehn | Viertel nach acht
  • 15:30 | fünfzehn Uhr dreißig | halb vier
  • 16:45 | sechzehn Uhr fünfundvierzig | Viertel vor fünf

Remember: Days, Months and Dates in Germany

Days: Montag (Monday), Dienstag (Tuesday), Mittwoch (Wednesday), Donnerstag (Thursday), Freitag (Friday), Samstag (Saturday), Sonntag (Sunday). Months: Januar, Februar, März, April, Mai, Juni, Juli, August, September, Oktober, November, Dezember. Dates: use a dot + am (am 14. Mai = on 14 May, am 3. Oktober = on 3 October).

Exercise halb-drei-test

  • Task: When do Peter and Lukas meet if Lukas says 'halb drei'?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ 2:30 p.m. (14:30) – halb drei = halfway to three, so 2:30 p.m. (14:30).
    • ✗ 3:30 p.m. (15:30) – 3:30 p.m. is 'halb vier' (halfway to four) in German.
    • ✗ 3:00 p.m. (15:00) – 3:00 p.m. is simply 'drei Uhr' without 'halb'.
  • Solution: 2:30 p.m. (14:30)
  • Explanation: In German, 'halb' always means 30 minutes before the named hour.

Exercise uhrzeit-paare

  • Task: Match each everyday German time to the right digital clock time.
  • Answer format: match each item on the left with one on the right
  • Solution: Viertel nach neun – 09:15; halb acht – 07:30; Viertel vor elf – 10:45; halb sechs – 17:30
  • Explanation: Viertel nach = :15, halb = :30 before the named hour, Viertel vor = :45.

Exercise zeit-datum-wahl

  • Task: Choose the right preposition (um, am, im) for Peter's calendar.
  • Answer format: choose a word for each gap
  • How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters wrong, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation wrong
  • Items (start → solution):
  1. Football practice is on Monday. → Das Fußballtraining ist am Montag.
  2. Class starts at 8:00. → Der Unterricht beginnt um 8:00 Uhr.
  3. Peter's birthday is on 14 May. → Peter hat am 14. Mai Geburtstag.
  4. The autumn break is in October. → Die Ferien sind im Oktober.
  • Explanation: Remember: um + clock time, am + day/date, im + month.
  • How to help: the app checks word by word against the solutions above and names mistakes of form (e.g. accents) with the child's words. Do not give the solution: ask about the rule (ending, gender, tense) or about a similar sentence of the page. If the app takes a version of the child's as wrong that you think is right, tell the child: the app knows only the versions above.
  • Link: ../?via=tutor#zeit-datum-wahl
