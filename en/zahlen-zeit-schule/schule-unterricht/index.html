In Herr Braun's Class

After this page you can name classroom objects with their German articles, name core school subjects, and politely ask for help in class. Herr Braun explains a task quickly, and Peter misses a word.

Exercise

Peter wants Herr Braun to say the sentence again (wiederholen = repeat). Which question fits best?

  • Können Sie das bitte wiederholen? right answer

    Polite with formal Sie and exact when you want something repeated.

  • Ich habe keine Zeit.

    That means 'I have no time' and does not ask for a repeat.

  • Auf Wiedersehen, Herr Braun!

    That says goodbye at the end of class.

With 'Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?' and 'Wie sagt man … auf Deutsch?' you can handle any lesson.

Classroom Objects, Subjects and Phrases

Every German noun has a fixed article: der (blue), das (green), or die (red). Always learn each noun together with its article!

Classroom objects, school subjects, and classroom phrases
GermanEnglish
der Stift / das Heft / das Buchthe pen / the notebook / the book
die Tafel / der Stundenplanthe board / the timetable
Mathe / Deutsch / Englisch / Sport / GeschichteMaths / German / English / PE / History
Wie sagt man … auf Deutsch?How do you say … in German?
Ich verstehe das nicht.I don't understand that.
