Hallo, Lindenberg! First Days in Germany

Peter (12) moves to Lindenberg, Germany with his family for a year. Follow Peter through his first greetings, du vs. Sie, forms, numbers, times, school phrases, signs, and short messages at CEFR A1.

Start: Hello and Goodbye: First Greetings

Contents

  1. Arriving, Greetings and Forms

    Peter arrives at Lindenberg station, meets the Weber family, learns du vs. Sie, fills in a form, and introduces himself in Year 6.

    Self-assessment

    1. Hello and Goodbye: First Greetings Greet people, say goodbye, and give your name, age, and home country in Germany.
    2. Informal du vs. Formal Sie Choose appropriately between informal du and formal Sie in Germany.
    3. Everyday Politeness in Germany Use typical politeness phrases for thanking, asking, apologizing, and meals in Germany.
    4. Filling in a Simple Form Understand common fields on a German registration form and fill them in accurately.
    5. Introducing Yourself in Class Introduce yourself in five connected sentences about your name, origin, age, home, and hobbies.
  2. Numbers, Time, Dates, Announcements and School

    Peter buys tickets and bread rolls, reads his timetable and calendar, uses classroom phrases, and understands signs and short station announcements.

    Self-assessment

    1. Numbers, Prices and Phone Numbers Understand German numbers, read prices in euros and cents, and share phone numbers.
    2. Time, Days, Months and Dates Tell formal and informal time in Germany and name days of the week, months, and dates.
    3. School and Classroom Phrases Name classroom objects and school subjects and use essential German classroom phrases.
    4. Reading Signs and Opening Hours Extract key facts from everyday German signs, opening hours, and public notices.
    5. Understanding Short Announcements Pick out key details like platform number, time, and delay from short German announcements.
  3. Short Messages and First Mediation

    Peter reads and writes short messages with Lukas and Nadia and relays key facts from a German poster to an English-speaking friend.

    Self-assessment

    1. What You Need Here (recap) Quick refresher before writing messages: the verb loves spot #2!
    2. Reading Short Messages Understand short text messages, postcards, and everyday invitations in German.
    3. Writing Short Messages Write short invitations, replies, and simple personal messages in German.
    4. Help Alex Read a German Poster Pass on key facts (time, place, price) from a German school-concert poster in English.
