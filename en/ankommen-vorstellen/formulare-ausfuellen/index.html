Signing Up for FC Lindenberg

Lukas brings Peter along to football practice at FC Lindenberg Jugend! To join the team, Peter fills in his sign-up card — and German addresses and dates follow a different order from English.

Exercise

Which format follows the German rule for Peter's address in Lindenberg?

  • Lindenstraße 12, 35037 Lindenberg right answer

    In Germany, street + house number come first, then the 5-digit postal code (PLZ) + town.

  • 12 Lindenstraße, Lindenberg 35037

    That is the English order (house number first, ZIP code last).

  • 35037, 12 Lindenstraße

    The PLZ always stands right before the town name, never before the street.

On German forms, the house number follows the street name and the 5-digit PLZ comes right before the town.

Fill In Every Field

Exercise

Which entry for classmate Nadia belongs in which form field?

Choose the match for each entry.

Right answer: Vorname – Nadia; PLZ – 35037; Wohnort – Lindenberg; Geburtsdatum – 08.11.2013

Vorname = first name, Nachname = last name, PLZ = 5-digit postal code, Wohnort = town.

Exercise

The German date of birth '04.07.2014' means 4 July 2014 (not 7 April).

Right answer: The statement is true.

Correct: in Germany the day (04) always comes first, then the month (07 = July), then the year.

Exercise

Fill in the missing German form-field labels.

Hint

All German form labels are nouns, so each one starts with a capital letter.

  1. First name: Nadia → …: Nadia
  2. Last name: Kaya → …: Kaya
  3. Town / City: Lindenberg → …: Lindenberg
Solution
  1. Vorname: Nadia
  2. Nachname: Kaya
  3. Wohnort: Lindenberg
