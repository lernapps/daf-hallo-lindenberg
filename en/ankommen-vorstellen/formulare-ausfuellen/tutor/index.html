Exercise formular-adresse

  • Task: Which format follows the German rule for Peter's address in Lindenberg?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Lindenstraße 12, 35037 Lindenberg – In Germany, street + house number come first, then the 5-digit postal code (PLZ) + town.
    • ✗ 12 Lindenstraße, Lindenberg 35037 – That is the English order (house number first, ZIP code last).
    • ✗ 35037, 12 Lindenstraße – The PLZ always stands right before the town name, never before the street.
  • Solution: Lindenstraße 12, 35037 Lindenberg
  • Explanation: On German forms, the house number follows the street name and the 5-digit PLZ comes right before the town.

Exercise formular-felder

  • Task: Which entry for classmate Nadia belongs in which form field?
  • Answer format: match each item on the left with one on the right
  • Solution: Vorname – Nadia; PLZ – 35037; Wohnort – Lindenberg; Geburtsdatum – 08.11.2013
  • Explanation: Vorname = first name, Nachname = last name, PLZ = 5-digit postal code, Wohnort = town.
  • Link: ../?via=tutor#formular-felder

Exercise formular-datum

  • Task: The German date of birth '04.07.2014' means 4 July 2014 (not 7 April).
  • Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
  • Solution: The statement is true.
  • Explanation: Correct: in Germany the day (04) always comes first, then the month (07 = July), then the year.
  • Link: ../?via=tutor#formular-datum

Exercise formular-luecken

  • Task: Fill in the missing German form-field labels.
  • Answer format: fill in the gaps in German
  • How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters right with a hint, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation ignored
  • Items (start → solution):
  1. First name: Nadia → Vorname: Nadia
  2. Last name: Kaya → Nachname: Kaya (also right: Nachname | Familienname)
  3. Town / City: Lindenberg → Wohnort: Lindenberg (also right: Wohnort | Ort)
  • The app's hint: All German form labels are nouns, so each one starts with a capital letter.
  • Explanation: The form is filed in Peter's Notebook. On the final page of Chapter 1, Peter introduces himself aloud in his new Klasse 6.
  • How to help: the app checks word by word against the solutions above and names mistakes of form (e.g. accents) with the child's words. Do not give the solution: ask about the rule (ending, gender, tense) or about a similar sentence of the page. If the app takes a version of the child's as wrong that you think is right, tell the child: the app knows only the versions above.
